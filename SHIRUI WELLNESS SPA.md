# SHIRUI WELLNESS SPA
# COMPLETE IMAGE & VISUAL ASSET GENERATION SPECIFICATION

This section is mandatory.

Do not randomly source images.

Do not use unrelated stock photographs.

Do not use images with inconsistent lighting, interiors, skin tones, uniforms or visual styles.

The complete Shirui Wellness Spa website must look as though every photograph and render came from **one professionally art-directed luxury wellness campaign**.

All visuals should tell one consistent story.

---

# 1. MASTER VISUAL DIRECTION

Every generated visual should follow this art direction unless a section specifically overrides it.

## Overall aesthetic

Premium boutique wellness spa in Hyderabad.

The environment should combine:

- dark natural wood
- warm beige limestone
- textured plaster walls
- muted bronze/champagne accents
- soft indirect architectural lighting
- cream and taupe fabrics
- minimal greenery
- refined treatment furniture
- elegant towels
- understated natural details

The spa must feel expensive but believable.

It should NOT resemble:

- a five-star hotel lobby
- a beauty salon
- a hospital
- a yoga studio
- a nightclub
- a cheap massage center
- a generic Bali resort
- a tropical beach spa

The aesthetic should feel appropriate for a premium urban wellness business in Hyderabad.

---

# 2. COLOR GRADING FOR ALL IMAGES

Maintain the same cinematic grade throughout.

Use:

warm amber highlights

neutral natural skin tones

deep brown-black shadows

cream whites

muted green botanical accents

warm stone

low saturation

soft highlight roll-off

moderate cinematic contrast

Do NOT use:

orange skin

extreme teal-and-orange grading

purple light

blue neon

hot pink

high saturation

washed-out white spa photography

---

# 3. LIGHTING STYLE

Primary lighting:

soft indirect warm lighting.

Color temperature:

approximately 3000K–3400K appearance.

Use:

hidden LED lighting

wall washers

table lamps

soft directional highlights

occasional candle warmth

Avoid visible harsh ceiling lights.

Images should retain shadow detail.

The mood should feel:

private

quiet

warm

restorative.

---

# 4. CAMERA LANGUAGE

The complete website should feel photographed with the same camera system.

Simulated camera:

full-frame professional camera.

Lens range:

35mm

50mm

85mm depending on scene.

Use:

natural depth of field

controlled cinematic bokeh

straight architectural lines for interiors

subtle shallow depth of field for detail shots

Do not use extreme wide-angle distortion.

Avoid fisheye appearance.

---

# 5. HUMAN SUBJECT CONSISTENCY

Use primarily adult Indian subjects.

Subjects should appear approximately:

28–45 years old.

Represent both men and women across the website where appropriate.

The visual identity should feel connected to Hyderabad without turning into a stereotype.

Do not exaggerate:

traditional clothing

jewellery

cultural props

skin smoothing

model-like glamour poses.

Guests should look like real premium wellness customers.

---

# 6. THERAPIST CONSISTENCY

Whenever therapists appear:

use professional adult Indian wellness therapists.

Uniform:

solid warm beige, taupe, charcoal or muted olive spa uniform.

No hospital scrubs.

No bright logos.

Hair neat and professional.

Hands clean and natural.

No jewellery while performing massage.

Maintain the SAME therapist visual identity across multiple images where possible.

If image generation supports character references:

create one therapist character reference first and reuse it.

---

# 7. GUEST CONSISTENCY

When a single guest appears across connected scenes, use a reference image or character consistency function.

Example story sequence:

guest arriving

guest entering treatment room

guest receiving massage

guest relaxing afterward

should look like the SAME person.

This dramatically improves premium brand consistency.

Every massage photograph must be tasteful and professional.

The guest must always be appropriately covered.

large cream spa towel

excessive cleavage
